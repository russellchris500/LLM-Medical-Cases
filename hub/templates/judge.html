AI judge - an LLM grader, trusted only where it matches you

A judge is one AI model plus the study's grading prompt. It sees what a human grader sees (case, rubric, blinded answer text) and returns the same Covered / Missed judgments plus the risk and approach questions; the 0/1/2 score is computed by the same rule. Every verdict on an answer a physician has graded is checked item by item against that grade: a verdict is accepted only when every rubric item matches every physician's judgment on that answer. Agreeing on the final score by accident is not enough.
